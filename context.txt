⚡ How to speed up training (quick playbook)


Press F (AI‑Fast).
→ Each tick runs many steps (default 20).
→ Press + to push to 50–100 steps/tick for big gains.


Press G to turn grid OFF.
→ Less drawing = higher throughput.


Keep it training for 1k–5k episodes.
→ Watch Avg50 trend in the info bar.
→ Press A to see greedy performance.
→ Press S to save the learned Q‑table.


For even faster runs: reduce GRID_W, GRID_H to e.g., 12 x 10.
→ Simpler state space → quicker convergence.


Optional: Lower TICK_MS from 50 → 20 ms to tick more often.
→ Or just rely on Fast bursts for better CPU efficiency.


If learning plateaus, switch back to AI‑Train for a bit (re‑introduces per‑tick rhythm) or bump epsilon slightly by toggling back to AI‑Fast (the code already resets epsilon from 0 to 0.5 when you return to training modes).
